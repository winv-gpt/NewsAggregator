Common questions

How do I get Ken’s Club back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the shop in the game, and tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

How do I cancel Ken’s Club?

iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Ken’s Sushi. Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel the subscription.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or Google through your Google Play order history.

I bought wasabi, hints or yen and didn’t get them.

Check your connection and reopen the shop. If they still haven’t arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the order number from your receipt.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Your game is saved on your phone. Ken’s Club can be restored on a new phone, but levels, wasabi, hints and yen stay with the old phone, so use them before you switch.

Why do I see a consent message or a tracking question?

The free game shows ads from Google. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first, and on iPhone Apple asks whether apps may track you. Saying no still lets you play; you just see ads that aren’t personalised. In Europe you can change your choice in the shop under Ad privacy choices. Members of Ken’s Club see no ads at all.

Can I change my chef name in the rankings?

Names are chosen by the game, so nobody can post anything unkind. They can’t be changed at the moment.

How do I delete my information?

Delete the app to remove your game from your phone. To remove your ranking record too, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} with your chef name. Our Privacy Policy has the details.
