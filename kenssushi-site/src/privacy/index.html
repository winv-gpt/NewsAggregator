1. The short version

  • Your game progress, settings and coins are kept on your phone.
  • If you play online, the game shares your scores in the rankings under a random player ID and a preset chef name, such as “Hana 42”. Other players can see that name, your scores and your league.
  • Apple or Google take your payments. We never see your card details.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, Google asks for your consent first, and on iPhone Apple asks whether apps may track you. Ken’s Club members see no ads.
  • We don’t sell your information for money, and the website uses no cookies and no analytics.

2. What stays on your phone

The app keeps your game on your device: your level, takings, fish book, station improvements, wasabi, hints, yen for station improvements, settings (such as sound and music) and the date you last played. We don’t receive this. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Reminders (for example, when the week’s league is about to end) are scheduled on your phone by the app itself, only if you allow notifications. No notification service or push token is involved, and nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game or in your phone’s settings.

The picture you can make to challenge a friend is created on your phone and only goes where you choose to share it.

4. Ads and your choices

Ken’s Sushi is free, and ads help pay for it: short videos you can choose to watch for a reward (such as a wasabi or a cup of tea), and an occasional ad between levels. Members of Ken’s Club see no ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier (the IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks you before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time: open the shop in the game and tap Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, Apple asks whether Ken’s Sushi may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier isn’t shared. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

If you decline personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are chosen without your advertising identifier.

5. The website

kenssushi.com is a plain website with no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. Our fonts are served from our own site, not by a third party. Like every website, the company that hosts it keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to deliver the site and keep it secure. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply.

6. Why we use information (legal bases)

Where laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we rely on:

  • Performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Use): running the game, the rankings and leagues, and delivering purchases.
  • Consent: personalised ads and tracking, which you can refuse or withdraw.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalised ads to keep the game free, keeping the game and rankings fair and secure, and answering your messages. You can object to these (see section 8).
  • Legal obligations: for example, keeping records the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Game progress on your phone: until you delete the app.
  • Rankings records: the app sends only your last 14 days of daily takings. We automatically delete a player’s ranking record after 12 months without play.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide purchases and meet tax and accounting laws.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own retention policies.
  • Emails to us: up to two years after we last hear from you, unless we must keep them longer.

8. Your rights, and deleting your information

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use any of these rights, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. We reply within one month.

To delete everything: uninstall the app to remove your game from your phone. To remove your ranking record too, email us your chef name (shown in the rankings) and roughly when you last played; because there are no accounts, this is how we find your record. Your Ken’s Club subscription is managed by Apple or Google; deleting the app doesn’t cancel it (see Support).

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Ken’s Sushi has no violence or chat and is suitable for all ages, but it isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. Players can’t type messages or names, so nobody can share personal details through the game. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

11. US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you have the rights described in section 8. We don’t sell personal information for money. When personalised ads are allowed, sharing an advertising identifier with Google may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising under some state laws. To opt out, choose “Ask App Not to Track” on iPhone, or delete your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation on Android; you can also email us. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights.

12. Security

Information sent by the app travels encrypted. Each player’s ranking record can only be changed by that player’s app. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to a minimum.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or on this website before they take effect.
